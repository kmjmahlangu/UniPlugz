About UniPlugz

News that students can relate to

UNIPLUGZ Is a student focused news and information platform dedicated to keeping students informed, connected and prepared for success. We aim to bridge the gap between students and the opportunities available to them. By helping students stay informed, we want to support better decision made by them, greater awareness and more meaningful participation in student life.

WHAT WE COVER

We update students about all the topics that are related to them, this includes funding opprtunities, bursaries, everything that s happening at the campus and the graduations
