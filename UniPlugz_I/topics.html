Funding

Find financial support to help you access and continue your university education. This section covers government funding schemes such as NSFAS, university bursaries, merit based scholarships, private sponsorships and other financial aid programmes.

Learn about eligibility requirements, application procedures, supporting documents, application deadlines and how to check your funding status. You can also find guidance on funding appeals, renewals and what to do when your funding application is unsuccessful.

Fees

Understand the financial responsibilities that come with studying at university. Explore tuition fees, registration costs, residence fees, additional academic charges and payment arrangements.

Find information about fee statements, outstanding balances, payment deadlines, fee blocks, refunds and student account management. This section also helps you understand how to handle financial holds, query incorrect charges and identify the university departments responsible for student fee enquiries.

Accommodation

Explore student housing options and learn how to secure suitable accommodation during your university studies. Find information about university residences, private student accommodation, accredited housing, application procedures, residence allocations and accommodation fees.

Understand what to consider before signing a lease, including rental agreements, deposits, utilities, safety, transport and facilities. Get guidance on residence rules, moving in, maintenance requests and what to do when you need alternative accommodation.

Campus Life

University is more than attending lectures and completing assignments. Discover the different experiences, activities and communities that contribute to student life. Explore student societies, campus organisations, sports, cultural activities, entertainment, social events and student leadership opportunities.

Find information about campus facilities, student support services, health and wellness resources, academic support, campus safety and ways to get involved in university communities.

Graduation

Prepare for one of the most important milestones in your academic journey. Find out about graduation eligibility, outstanding academic requirements, qualification completion, graduation applications and ceremony arrangements. Explore information about graduation dates, academic dress, guest tickets, certificates, degree collection and academic transcripts.

Get guidance on outstanding fees, administrative clearances, postponements and what to expect before, during and after your graduation ceremony.

Opportunities

Discover opportunities that can help you develop professionally, gain experience and prepare for life beyond university. Explore internships, learnerships, vacation work, part-time employment, graduate programmes, volunteering, competitions, entrepreneurship programmes and industry placements.

Find information about application requirements, CV preparation, portfolios, interviews, professional networking and career development. You can also discover opportunities to build practical skills, connect with employers and gain experience while completing your studies.
